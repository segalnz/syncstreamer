# Plan: Fix seq_gaps spike on track change + crossfade gap concealment

## Problem 1: Large seq_gaps on track change

**Root cause**: `last_seq` and `first` in `wifi_rx_task()` are local static variables never reset across streams. When a new stream starts (sequence resets to 0), the gap calculation `pkt.sequence - expected` produces a huge negative wraparound, adding millions to `g_seq_gaps`.

**Fix**: Add `network_receiver_stream_reset()` that resets `last_seq=0`, `first=true`, `g_seq_gaps=0`. Call it from:
- `ctrl_rx_task` on `STREAM_START`  
- `state_machine_tick` on `IDLE → ACQUIRING`  
- `ctrl_rx_task` on `STREAM_STOP` (also safe)

## Problem 2: Small seq gaps causing audio dropouts

**Root cause**: Single-packet loss (~5.3ms = 256 frames) triggers sample-hold gap concealment in `Resampler.cpp`. The resampler holds the last valid sample for 256+ consecutive frames, producing an audible click/dropout. The PLL also overcorrects due to sudden occupancy change.

**Fix**: Replace simple sample-hold with crossfade gap concealment:
- Track consecutive concealed frames count (`s_conceal_count` static in `Resampler.cpp`)
- On entering concealment: fade from current level to -60dB over 240 frames (5ms)
- On exiting concealment: fade back in over 240 frames
- Crossfade is a simple linear ramp multiplication — no additional buffers needed
